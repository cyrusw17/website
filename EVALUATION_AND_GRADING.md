# GroundWork - Web: Version Evaluation & Grading

## Grading Criteria (Each out of 10, Total: 100 points for A+)

1. **Positioning Clarity** - Visitor knows niche/specialization instantly
2. **Value Proposition** - Clear benefit within 3 seconds
3. **Trust Signals** - Proof, testimonials, results visible
4. **Call-to-Action Strength** - Clear, compelling, multiple paths
5. **Mobile UX** - Flawless mobile experience
6. **Load Speed** - Under 2 seconds (optimized code)
7. **Clarity** - Zero ambiguity, scannable, direct
8. **Social Proof** - Reviews, case studies, specific results
9. **Friction Reduction** - Easy conversion, minimal barriers
10. **Objection Handling** - FAQ addresses real concerns
