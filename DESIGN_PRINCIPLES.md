## Research-Backed Design Principles

### 1. SPECIALIZED POSITIONING (2-3x Pricing Power)
- **NOT:** "We build websites for businesses"
- **YES:** "We build high-converting websites exclusively for automotive detailing companies"
- Specialization commands premium rates and eliminates price comparison
- Demonstrates deep understanding of detailing industry pain points

### 2. DUAL CONVERSION PATHS
- **Primary CTA:** Book a 30-Minute Strategy Call
- **Secondary CTA:** Skip the Call - Order Now & Text/Email
- Place phone number (270-801-9780) prominently everywhere
- Both CTAs visible above fold on every page

### 3. CLARITY OVER CREATIVITY (Anti-AI Slop)
- Visitor knows what we do, who we serve, and what to do next in <5 seconds
- No vague language, no generic stock photos, no walls of text
- Every element serves conversion
- Short paragraphs, scannable bullets, clear hierarchy

### 4. AUTOMOTIVE DETAILING INDUSTRY PAIN POINTS TO ADDRESS
**Their Current Problems:**
- Slow-loading websites that lose mobile visitors
- Generic templates that don't showcase their work
- No online booking (losing customers to competitors)
- Hidden pricing forcing unnecessary calls
- Poor mobile experience
- No service-specific landing pages
- Weak SEO - not ranking for "ceramic coating [city]"
- No review integration or social proof
- Confusing navigation

**Our Solutions (Show We Understand):**
- Fast, mobile-optimized sites (2s load time)
- Before/after galleries that sell services
- Integrated booking systems
- Transparent pricing guidance
- Service-specific pages for SEO
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Review showcase integration
- Clear conversion paths

### 5. TRUST SIGNALS & PROOF
- Before/after examples of detailing sites we've built
- Specific results: "Increased bookings by 340%"
- Testimonials from detailing shop owners
- Number of detailing companies served
- Screenshots showing booking increases
- Speed test comparisons
- Mobile responsiveness demonstrations

### 6. MOBILE-FIRST DESIGN
- 70%+ of traffic will be mobile
- Tap-to-call buttons prominent
- Forms with minimal fields
- No pinch-to-zoom required
- Touch-friendly spacing
- Fast load times on 4G

### 7. CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION
**Above the Fold Must Include:**
- Clear headline: WHO we serve + WHAT outcome
- One-sentence benefit statement
- Primary CTA button
- Tap-to-call phone number
- Secondary "skip call" CTA
- High-quality image showing results

**Page Structure:**
- Hero: Instant clarity + dual CTAs
- Problem/Outcome: Their pain + our transformation
- Proof: Results, testimonials, case studies
- Services: Clear packages/options
- Process: How we work (3-4 steps max)
- FAQ: Handle objections
- Final CTA: Strong conversion block

### 8. PRICING PSYCHOLOGY
- Show package tiers (Good/Better/Best)
- Anchor with premium option first
- Make middle option look reasonable
- Include "starting from" pricing
- Frame in ROI terms, not cost
- "Investment" not "price"

### 9. CALL-TO-ACTION BEST PRACTICES
**Strong CTAs:**
- "Book Your Free Website Audit" (specific, valuable)
- "Get Your Detailing Website Quote in 24 Hours"
- "Skip the Call - Order Your Site Now"

**Weak CTAs (AVOID):**
- "Learn More"
- "Contact Us"
- "Submit"

### 10. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- **Load Time:** Under 2 seconds
- **Mobile Score:** 95+ on Google PageSpeed
- **No unnecessary animations** (kills conversion)
- **Compressed images:** Under 200KB each
- **Clean, semantic HTML**
- **No bloated frameworks**

## Copy Framework

### Headlines
- Focus on OUTCOME not process
- Include "automotive detailing" for clarity
- Promise specific result

### Body Copy
- Short sentences
- One idea per paragraph
- Use "you" not "we"
- Bullets over paragraphs
- Specific numbers/results
- Active voice only

### CTAs
- Start with action verb
- Be specific about what happens next
- Include time expectation
- Reduce friction/risk
