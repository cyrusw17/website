# Complete Website Redesign — GroundWork Web

## What Changed and Why

### The Problem
The previous versions had these critical issues:
- **Not inviting or friendly**: Harsh black/white designs felt cold and uninviting
- **No trust factors**: Missing testimonials, stats, guarantees, and social proof
- **No clear CTAs**: Call-to-action buttons were not prominent or frequent enough
- **Lacked warmth**: Design didn't build confidence or encourage action

### The Solution
Complete redesign of all three versions with conversion-focused design principles:
