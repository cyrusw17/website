FAQ

Common questions

Why is there no monthly fee?

Because you shouldn't have to pay forever for a website you'll never own. We charge once for the design and build, then hand over everything — domain, files, and hosting login — so you're never locked in.

What happens after the first year of free hosting?

You can renew hosting with us for a small annual fee (around $150/yr) or move it anywhere you like — it's your website.

What if I need changes later?

Minor updates can be done yourself with the simple editor we set up, or we're happy to make changes for a small one-time fee — no subscription required.

How long does it take?

Typically 2–3 weeks from kickoff, depending on how quickly you send us photos and feedback.

Do I need to provide photos?

Great photos make a huge difference. If you don't have professional shots, we'll guide you on what to capture with just your phone.
